About Open Source Stats

For those who are curious

What's this about?

This is a free and open source website for sharing data visualizations and datasets. It pledges to remain free and ad-free to provide the best user experience.

How to contribute?

Users can post their visualizations and datasets in their respective sections. They will be made publicly available on the website after an AI content moderation process. Users can also post in forums, creating comment threads and responding to different topics.

How to become popular?

The visibility of a post is mainly determined by the number of likes and interactions (e.g. comments). To prevent spamming, we have trained a machine learning model to detect dishonest behavior, which will be reviewed and handled by human moderators. Penalty might involve deletion of the post.

Any jobs?

We are an free and open source website. We don't make any revenue so we don't offer paid employment. If you would like to contribute your expertise - in software development, translation, marketing etc. - you can contact us at OpenSourceStats@gmail.com

If you're a developer, you're also welcome to submit a pull request to our GitHub project. We recommend working on the TODO items in the issues tab to improve your chances of getting your work merged.
